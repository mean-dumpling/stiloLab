Essential information about the processing of personal data.

General information

This notice describes how the website processes the personal data of its visitors in accordance with Regulation (EU) 2016/679 and the applicable data protection laws.

Data controller

The data controller is Stilo Lab. For any request concerning the protection of personal data, please contact farmastilosrl@gmail.com.

Types of data processed

During normal browsing, technical and usage data may be collected, including IP address, browser type, operating system, pages visited, date and time of the request and other information necessary for the correct operation of the website.

Providing data by email, contact forms or messaging services is optional. Voluntarily sending a request results in the collection of the data provided by the user and of the information needed to reply.

Purposes of processing

Personal data may be processed to:

  • enable browsing and the correct operation of the website;
  • ensure system security and prevent unlawful use;
  • answer requests for information or assistance;
  • comply with legal obligations or requests from competent authorities;
  • protect the rights and legitimate interests of the controller.

Legal basis

Depending on the circumstances, processing is based on taking steps requested by the data subject, compliance with legal obligations and the controller's legitimate interest in managing, protecting and improving the website and answering communications received.

Processing methods and security

Data are processed using electronic and digital tools in accordance with the principles of lawfulness, fairness, transparency, relevance and data minimisation. Appropriate technical and organisational measures are used to prevent unauthorised access, loss, disclosure or unlawful use of data.

Data retention

Data are retained only for as long as necessary to achieve the purposes for which they were collected and, where applicable, for the period required by law. Requests sent by users may be retained for the time needed to manage them and protect the controller's rights.

Recipients of data

Data may be processed by authorised persons and by providers supporting the operation of the website and its services, including hosting, email, technical support and communication service providers. Data are not made public or sold to third parties for commercial purposes.

International data transfers

If a provider processes data outside the European Economic Area, the transfer will take place in accordance with applicable law and on the basis of the safeguards required by Regulation (EU) 2016/679.

Cookies and similar technologies

The website only uses technical tools necessary for its operation and for storing user preferences. No advertising or profiling cookies are used. Preferences can be deleted at any time through the browser settings.

External services and links

The website may contain links to or integrate resources provided by third parties. The use of these services may involve the processing of technical data under their respective privacy notices. The controller is not responsible for the processing practices of external websites or services.

Data subject rights

Where provided by law, data subjects may request access to, correction or deletion of their data, restriction of processing and data portability, and may object to processing or withdraw any consent given without affecting the lawfulness of processing carried out before withdrawal.

Requests may be sent to farmastilosrl@gmail.com. Data subjects may also lodge a complaint with the Italian Data Protection Authority.

Changes to this notice

The controller reserves the right to update this notice at any time. Changes will be published on this page and will take effect from the date of publication.
