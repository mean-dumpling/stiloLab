About us

Stilo Lab is the line of food supplements born from the daily experience of Farmacia Stilo, at the counter, alongside people.

From the pharmacy to the laboratory

Every day, in the pharmacy, we listen to concrete needs: someone looking for support for the urinary tract, someone wanting to cleanse after a period of excess, someone facing months of stress and fatigue. Stilo Lab was created to answer those requests with formulas designed by pharmacists, with no superfluous ingredients.

We do not believe in endless lists of components in token doses: we believe in a few ingredients, well chosen, in amounts that make sense.

Our approach

We do not simply add "trending" ingredients. Every extract is chosen by assessing the quality of the raw material, standardisation, the scientific literature, bioavailability and compatibility with the other components of the formula, with the aim of arriving at a rational, coherent composition.

Every formula comes out of a structured development process:

  • Review of the scientific literature — every choice starts from the available evidence.
  • Selection of standardised extracts — for consistent quality over time.
  • Assessment of assays — we verify the actual concentration of the active principles.
  • Setting effective doses — doses consistent with the literature, never token ones.
  • Checking synergies — we assess compatibility and synergies between the ingredients.

A range that grows with you

The Stilo Lab range is constantly evolving: new formulas come out of conversations with the people who walk into the pharmacy. If you cannot find what you are looking for, talk to us — the next formula could come from your own need.
